# My Favorite Series: Breaking Bad

> *"It is never too late to be what you might have been."*

## Overview
I enjoy watching **Breaking Bad** because it shows the reality that life doesn't always turn people into who they want to be. Sometimes, circumstances, desperation, pride, and the choices we make slowly shape us into someone we never thought we'd become.

## Key Highlights
- **Main Characters / Cast:** Walter White (Bryan Cranston), Jesse Pinkman (Aaron Paul)
- **Genre & Vibe:** Crime Drama, Psychological Thriller, Neo-Western
- **Best Episode or Arc:** *Ozymandias* (Season 5, Episode 14)

## Top Episodes to Watch
1. **Season 1, Episode 1 (`Pilot`)** – *The beginning of Walter White's transformation*
2. **Season 4, Episode 13 (`Face Off`)** – *The ultimate showdown and turning point*
3. **Season 5, Episode 14 (`Ozymandias`)** – *The devastating climax of Walter's empire*
4. **Season 5, Episode 16 (`Felina`)** – *The unforgettable series finale*
